# AI-Assisted Development Project Operating Model 2.0
## Profile: SOLUTION


1. **Purpose.** SOLUTION governs durable solution, architecture, design and implementation-definition work where the primary accepted output is a solution contract rather than an executable product candidate.
2. **Authority.** The project shall declare one authoritative control plane for current solution state and decisions. Source material may remain authoritative externally when explicitly referenced.
3. **Bootstrap.** Recovery shall identify the current objective, accepted solution baseline, active decision or review boundary, blockers and next action without requiring chat history.
4. **Requirements traceability.** Material solution decisions shall be traceable to requirements, constraints, assumptions or explicit accepted trade-offs.
5. **Decision integrity.** Superseded decisions remain historical evidence. A later decision replaces them prospectively rather than rewriting their prior meaning.
6. **Validation.** Acceptance shall include proportionate review against architecture invariants, material requirements, interfaces, security/safety constraints and known limitations.
7. **External dependencies.** Material external contracts used for acceptance shall be pinned or otherwise identifiable strongly enough for later audit where reproducibility matters.
8. **Prototype boundary.** Prototype or proof-of-concept output is not production authority unless explicitly promoted through an applicable DEV or other implementation profile.
9. **Connected work.** Read-only discovery is distinct from mutation. Any connected mutation follows the same fail-closed target and authorization principles as CORE and the connected-mutation playbook.
10. **Closure.** An accepted solution baseline preserves its exact version/ref, decision lineage, material assumptions, unresolved limitations and the evidence supporting acceptance.
11. **Escalation.** If executable product development, candidate qualification or governed release becomes the primary objective, reclassify prospectively to DEV rather than silently extending SOLUTION.
