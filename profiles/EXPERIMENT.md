# AI-Assisted Development Project Operating Model 2.0
## Profile: EXPERIMENT

**Status:** NON-CANONICAL CANDIDATE

1. **Purpose.** EXPERIMENT governs bounded exploratory work intended to test a hypothesis, feasibility question, technology choice or uncertain approach.
2. **Hypothesis.** The experiment shall state the question or hypothesis, bounded scope and success/failure signal strongly enough to avoid post-hoc reinterpretation.
3. **Authority.** Persistent experiment state shall have one declared authority; chat-only state is insufficient for experiments expected to survive a session.
4. **Isolation.** Experiments shall not silently gain production authority. Connected mutation, if any, is isolated to explicitly authorized non-production targets by default.
5. **Proportional reproducibility.** Inputs, environment and outputs are recorded only to the degree needed to reproduce or evaluate the experiment's conclusion.
6. **Evidence.** Both positive and negative results are preserved when they materially inform the decision. Failed experiments are not rewritten into successful narratives.
7. **Time/scope bound.** The experiment should end when the defined question is answered, disproven, blocked or no longer worth pursuing.
8. **Promotion.** Reusable product, solution or document work discovered through an experiment is promoted prospectively into the appropriate profile with an explicit trusted starting boundary.
9. **No candidate semantics by default.** DEV candidate/release controls do not apply unless the experiment is explicitly reclassified.
10. **Closure.** Closure records the hypothesis, method at useful granularity, result, confidence/limitations and disposition: stop, repeat, promote or defer.
