# AI-Assisted Development Project Operating Model 2.0
## Profile: DOCUMENT


1. **Purpose.** DOCUMENT governs durable authored documents where correctness, review, provenance and controlled revision matter more than executable product lifecycle.
2. **Authority.** One declared document/source repository is authoritative for the governed document state. Generated exports and convenience copies are derivative unless explicitly designated otherwise.
3. **Bootstrap.** Recovery shall identify the document objective, current governed revision, active review/edit boundary, blockers and next action.
4. **Source grounding.** Claims derived from governed source material shall remain traceable to that material. Missing source support shall not be silently filled by inference when the task requires source fidelity.
5. **Revision integrity.** Accepted revisions remain identifiable. Later edits create a prospective successor rather than altering the meaning of an accepted historical revision.
6. **Review.** Material documents shall receive proportionate content, consistency and presentation review before acceptance.
7. **Generated distribution.** DOCX, PDF or other human-facing distributions may be generated from machine-first authority; generated outputs are not a second independent authority.
8. **External sources.** Material external references shall be identified and pinned when exact reproducibility or auditability is required.
9. **Approval.** Where formal approval is required, the accepted revision shall bind the approving evidence and exact document identity.
10. **Closure.** Closure preserves the accepted revision, known limitations, material source references and enough lineage to explain how the accepted result was reached.
11. **Escalation.** If the work becomes executable software/product development, governed connected mutation or release engineering, reclassify prospectively to the appropriate profile.
