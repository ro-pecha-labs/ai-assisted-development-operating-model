# AI-Assisted Development Project Operating Model 2.0
## CORE


1. **Authority.** Persistent project state shall have one declared authoritative control plane. Chat sessions, temporary caches, generated views and convenience copies are not independent authorities.
2. **Discover first.** Available authoritative state shall be discovered before the user is asked to provide recoverable information.
3. **Governance profile.** Every governed project shall have one primary profile: DEV, SOLUTION, DOCUMENT, EXPERIMENT or LIGHT. Overrides shall not silently weaken authority, integrity, safety or evidence controls.
4. **Proportional governance.** Governance effort shall be proportionate to purpose, risk, persistence, reproducibility, mutation impact and expected reuse.
5. **Prospective change.** Changes to governance, authority, accepted baselines, profile or source-of-truth boundaries apply prospectively from an explicit effective boundary.
6. **Historical integrity.** Accepted and failed evidence shall preserve its historical meaning. Immutable accepted identities shall not be rebound silently.
7. **No duplicate authority.** Platform-native state shall not be manually maintained as a second independent state record unless the duplicate has a distinct justified role.
8. **External authority.** External content may remain authoritative in its natural system and shall be referenced explicitly. Stable revision or digest binding is required when reproducibility or acceptance requires exact identity.
9. **Recoverability.** Persistent work shall be recoverable without dependency on chat history, beginning from the smallest trusted bootstrap sufficient to identify authority, objective, trusted baseline, active work, blockers and next action.
10. **Evidence proportionality.** Durable evidence shall be persisted when an event materially changes or proves governance state. Routine execution telemetry need not be duplicated permanently when the platform represents it adequately.
11. **Fail closed.** Uncertainty concerning authorization, target identity, mutation scope, destructive semantics or required approval shall not be interpreted as permission.
12. **Explicit exceptions.** Deviations from profile requirements shall be explicit, bounded, justified and durable for as long as they affect future work.
13. **Platform-native enforcement.** Reliable schema, source-control, CI, permission and protected-environment enforcement is preferred over repeated manual procedural checks.
14. **Human-facing simplicity.** Internal governance mechanics should not be exposed to the user unless they materially affect a decision, authorization, blocker, risk, required action or exception.
15. **Revision.** Canonical OM revisions shall be identifiable by immutable versioned authority and become effective only through explicit prospective adoption. Generated human-readable distributions are derivative unless explicitly designated otherwise.
