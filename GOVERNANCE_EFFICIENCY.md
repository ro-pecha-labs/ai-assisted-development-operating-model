# Governance Efficiency Guidance

**Status:** PROSPECTIVE GUIDANCE FOR OM 2.1 DEVELOPMENT

Governance is successful when it reduces total delivery/recovery/risk cost, not when it maximizes governance artifacts.

Projects may track, where useful:

- governance effort / total effort;
- recovery time from a fresh session;
- rework caused by stale or duplicated state;
- candidate failures caught before external mutation;
- CI minutes and expensive-runner minutes;
- duplicate execution count;
- manual governance artifact count;
- time from development-ready to accepted/released state;
- AI-context/token or credit consumption where measurable.

OM does not impose one universal percentage budget.

A project may declare its own budget or threshold. Persistent material overhead above that budget should trigger a simplification review before more ceremony is added.

Optimization shall not silently weaken fail-closed authorization, historical integrity, exact identity or required external controls.
